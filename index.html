Found auto-renew clause that would lock client for 24 months

Brand Deal

Identified perpetual, worldwide rights grab in paragraph 4 of a brand deal

One sentence gave the brand irrevocable ownership of all content created during the campaign. Narrowed to 12-month license, creator retains master rights.

Vendor Agreement

Flagged indemnification clause with zero liability cap on a $5K project

Section 8 had no dollar limit. A single client complaint could have triggered $150K+ in damages. Capped at 2x contract value.

Freelance Contract

Caught non-compete in Section 12 requiring 18 months of no competing work

Employment

Found IP assignment clause covering personal side projects

Contractor agreement claimed ownership of all work created "during the term," including personal projects. Scoped to client deliverables only.

Lease Agreement

Annual 5% compounding increase on top of maintenance fees. Would have cost $8,700 extra over 3 years. Negotiated to fixed 3% with a hard cap.
