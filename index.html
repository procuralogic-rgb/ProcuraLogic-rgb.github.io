SaaS Vendor Agreement — Sample Report

2 High
1 Medium
1 Low
Contract Type: SaaS Agreement
Value: $24,000/year
Term: 24 months
Risk Level: High

Findings

High Risk

Section 7.2: Uncapped indemnification

No liability limit. A $5K project could expose you to $150K+ in damages.

Recommendation: Cap liability at 2x annual contract value ($48K). Add mutual indemnification.

High Risk

Section 12.1: Broad IP assignment clause

Current language assigns all "work product created during the term" to the vendor, including your internal tools and processes.

Recommendation: Narrow scope to deliverables only. Add carve-out for pre-existing IP.

Medium Risk

Section 4.1: Auto-renewal on page 9

24-month renewal with a 15-day cancellation window. Easy to miss.

Recommendation: Renegotiate to 6-month term with 60-day notice window.

Low Risk

Section 9: Standard confidentiality

1-year post-termination NDA. Industry standard. No changes needed.

Bottom Line

Do not sign this contract as-is. The uncapped indemnification and IP assignment clauses create significant financial exposure. With the recommended changes, this becomes a standard, fair agreement. Estimated savings from renegotiation: $8,700 to $18,200 over the contract term.
